# Synvra

**Laboratory information management for ISO/IEC 17025 testing and calibration laboratories.**

Synvra LIMS keeps a laboratory's work in one controlled chain, from request to a certificate the
customer can verify, and records the evidence as the work happens.

- Sample-to-report workflow: request, receipt and chain of custody, testing, review and release
- Results bound to an approved method version and a serviceable instrument
- Independent review with four-eyes approval, electronic signatures and re-authentication on release
- An audit trail on every regulated action
- Certificates anyone can check by QR code, without an account

**Status:** a working system hosted on AWS, at pilot stage. Focus markets: Pakistan, Saudi Arabia
and the United Arab Emirates.

**Built with:** .NET 9 and ASP.NET Core, React and TypeScript, PostgreSQL, AWS and Terraform.

Our product repositories are private. To see Synvra LIMS working, book a demo at
[synvra.ai](https://synvra.ai).

Synvra supports ISO/IEC 17025-oriented workflows and evidence capture; accreditation remains the
laboratory's responsibility.
